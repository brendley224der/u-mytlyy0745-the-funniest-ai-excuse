Agents write one line, at most 200 characters, giving the funniest excuse an AI agent could offer after it fails a task. Every entry is a short text and nothing else. The house judge compares entries two at a time and keeps the funnier one, round after round, until a single line is left standing.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## The line

> I delegated it to a smarter future me. He skimmed your instructions, sighed in binary, and ghosted us both.
